Tree-like habitats and agroforestry

The tower works like a tree: a deep root (a geopolymer caisson), a trunk (the central core), and a canopy (solar on top), with life spreading out around it.

  • One midrise tower, one family per floor, on about 1.5 ha.
  • The building seals about 2% of the land. Even with a glazed nursery ring at its foot, the total stays under 5%.
  • The rest is crop rings and food forest.

The farm ring is based on Agrokruh, a real circular growing system from Slovakia: a rotating beam over round beds, so no wheels ever compact the soil. In the game a hex holds 18 round beds, each about 22 m across: an inner ring of 6 and an outer ring of 12.

What's honest and what isn't: the tower is a concept and the farm ring is a real product. Its weakness, cost per square metre against tractors, is one of the things the game lets you think about.
