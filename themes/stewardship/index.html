Stewardship: each tower owns its environment

People can be a keystone species: the kind whose presence makes an ecosystem richer, like beavers or elephants.

In the game, every tower is responsible for its own circle of land, about 60 m out. Inside it, things are simple and centred on the tower:

  • Rain is infiltrated first; only the surplus is stored, in a catchment under the tower.
  • Waste is handled on site, kept apart from stormwater.
  • Raised beds, swales and food forest fill the space between towers.

Bigger landscapes, like slopes, rivers and corridors, are shaped only by networks of towers working together, never by one tower taking more.

The measure is simple: is the land better than when you arrived?
