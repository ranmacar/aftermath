Open design

The things in the game should be buildable outside it.

  • The tower, pod and farm ring are modelled as parametric Onshape CAD, from the same numbers the game uses.
  • Designs are published openly as they become useful, starting small, with a planter ring for a garden.
  • Concepts are labelled as concepts and prototypes as untested.

Behind it is a principle we'd like to see in land rules too: permission by performance. Judge a home by what it does to the land, how little it seals and how much stays productive or wild over time, not by which zone it's in. If the land stops performing, the permission lapses.
