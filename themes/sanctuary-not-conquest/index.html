Sanctuary, not conquest

Most strategy games are about taking. This one is about keeping something alive.

  • There's no wave defense and no combat loop at the core.
  • Survivors you bring home are residents with lives, not units.
  • Your territory is the set of hexes your power and care actually reach (a connected graph), not colour painted on a map.
  • Other players and factions show up first as neighbours and traders.

The tension comes from scarcity, weather and time, and from the cities on the horizon that are still hungry.
