The long game: 100 billion people by 2500

A deliberately big number, to change the question. Not "how few people can the Earth bear?" but "how could many more people live here while the land gets richer?"

If every household lives in a tower that leaves 95% of its land growing, and towers form networks across regions, density stops meaning concrete. That's the long-term horizon the project points at.

It's a direction, not a forecast. The game plays out one hex and one generation at a time. The long game is the reason the small steps add up.
